## 2. Two corrections to the brief

1. **The fins.** The video does say that the fins of the Walton valve make it turn slightly at each rise (12:02–12:14). What it
   does not say is that the author's own fins work: he announces that he will file some, hopes for the rotation, says he does
   not know whether it will work and that he will show it off-camera (11:48–12:49). The lessons are right to call it
   « unproven » for the lab's valve. The faults are in F05.
2. **« Farm ».** The word never occurs in the video's captions; the farm of S7 (Réussir) is another installation (12 m fall,
   16 L/min, 380 m). See F29.
