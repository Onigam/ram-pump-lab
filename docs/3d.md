# 3D worlds

Two worlds built from `src/three/dims.ts` (metres, the only source: every constant says `reported`, `derived` or
`estimate`). `index.ts` exports `createSiteScene()` and `createRamScene()`; parts and hotspots use the frozen ids of
research §4 (7 + 18). Checked by `three.test.ts` (ids, floating or buried parts, explode, NaN, 300 m route, dimensions).

## Files

- `common.ts` lathe/hex/rod geometry, `FlowDots` (dots along a polyline), water material. `cut.ts` the section tool.
- `ram/` `model.ts` (one model, animation, used twice), `body.ts`, `inlet.ts`, `waste.ts` + `wastegeo.ts` (the video's
  valve), `chamber.ts` (check valve, air chamber), `fittings.ts`, `dial.ts`, `water.ts` (water, spray, Flux dots), `scene.ts`.
- `site/` `layout.ts` (ground, drive geometry, route), `terrain.ts`, `sky.ts`, `shadow.ts`, `vegetation.ts`, `pipes.ts`,
  `routes.ts`, `buildings.ts` (house, tower), `tank.ts` (tank, spring), `pit.ts`, `scene.ts`.

## Frames and scale decisions

- **Site**: X east, Y up, Z south, Y = 0 on the ram's inlet axis; the ground at the pit is 0.85 m. The hill rises north.
  Fall (7.5 m) and head (65 m) are drawn at true height; nothing is stretched vertically.
- **Delivery route**: its plan is drawn once (`DELIVERY_ROUTE`) and lies on the ground, so the pipe is 300 m (299 m, with
  riser and goose-neck) at H = 65 m and a few per cent longer or shorter for other heads (the state has no `deliveryLength`).
- **Compressed for legibility**: pipes are never thinner than 2.6 px on screen (vertex shader, true size when close); the
  valve stroke is drawn ×4 (`VALVE.strokeVisualScale`, max 30 mm), the check-valve disc lift ×2; the drive and delivery
  stubs of the ram world are 0.16 m and 0.12 m long; delivery-pipe dots travel at a symbolic speed (the real one is mm/s).
- **Ram world**: X along the body, Z towards the viewer, the section plane is z = 0. The ram instance in the pit is the same
  model, unregistered. The waste valve shown is n°1 whatever `valve` says; weight and chamber size are fixed (not in the state).
- **Waste valve** as in the video: base plate + welded 26×34 socket, reduction, 15×21 nipple with four ports, sleeve,
  thermowell guide (bore 10 mm, sleeves 9.9 mm); the stem (M6 rod, sleeves, countersunk head on a conical seat ring, fins nut)
  hangs on the counterweight, which lands on the guide, then stroke nut and lock nut on top (the video's final valve has only
  weight + lock nut: the catalogue's stroke nut is drawn between them).

## What `update(state, dt, view)` reads

| State                                                  | Effect                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `wasteValve`, `stroke`, `strokeCount`                  | stem, fins, nuts, weight lift (visual smoothing below); stem turns 14° per stroke, ≤160°/s |
| `deliveryValve`, `chamberPressure`, `airLevel`         | check disc and spring; gauge needle (damped, 0.22 s); water level in the chamber           |
| `wasteJetFlow`, `snifterFlow`                          | stream in the nipple and spray from the ports; snifter pin, air dots                       |
| `driveVelocity`, flows (`*FlowNow`, averages)          | Flux dots (signed), arrival at the tower, waste stream below the pit, tank overflow        |
| `tankLevel`, `sourceFlow`, `hour`                      | tank water, spring trickle, sun and sky (the day follows the clock)                        |
| `fall`, `driveLength`, `driveDiameter`, `deliveryHead` | terrain, tank, spring, pipes, tower and house follow (rebuilt ≤ every 50 ms, 4–11 ms)      |
| `view.cutaway`, `view.schematic`                       | section (ram world only), Flux overlays; `slowMo` is not applied again                     |

Stalled: the state stops, nothing else moves. While anything is exploded, wire or a container is hidden (assembly guide),
the valve is held in its rest pose (open, weight on the guide), water and sprays are hidden.

**Valve smoothing**: the state closes the valve in about one frame, so the drawn value follows it with time constants 12 ms
(closing) and 30 ms (opening): the slam lasts 2 to 3 frames and the reopening reads as a fall. The stroke itself is untouched.

**Explode**: every part moves along its mounting axis (stack parts up, the stem down, valves along X, site parts up by 5 to
30 m); nested children of `waste-valve` are relative to it.
