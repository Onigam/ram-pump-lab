# 3D worlds: the ram and the valve

Two device worlds built from `src/three/dims.ts` (metres, the only source: every constant says `reported`, `derived` or
`estimate`) with the frozen ids of `src/part-ids.ts` (research §11): `ram` 13 parts, `valve` 14. `index.ts` exports
`createRamScene()` and `createValveScene()` (each returns its `SceneModule` plus its `model`). The site and the catchment:
`docs/3d-site.md`. Checked by `ram.test.ts` and `valve.test.ts` (ids, floating or buried parts, explode, NaN, section caps,
interpenetration of the sections, and that both worlds hold the very same valve).

## Files

- `common.ts` lathe, hex nut, thread, `paperFloor`, `FlowDots`, water material. `cut.ts` the section tool (`CutKit`).
- `valve/` `model.ts` (`createWasteValveModel`: the valve and its animation), `parts.ts` (the 14 builders), `geo.ts` (derived
  sizes), `water.ts` (column, stream, spray), `explode.ts`, `bench.ts` (plinth, parallels, rule), `index.ts` (the world).
- `ram/` `model.ts` (the ram as one object, used by the `ram` world and, unregistered, by the site's pit), `body.ts`,
  `inlet.ts` (V1, strainer, inlet flange), `chamber.ts`, `fittings.ts` (gauge, snifter, V3, union, V2), `foundation.ts`,
  `water.ts`, `explode.ts`, `scene.ts`.

## One valve, two worlds

`createWasteValveModel({ cut, hardware, capId, floorAt })` builds the 14 parts once, in a frame whose origin is the centre of
the top face of the base plate (Y up, section plane z = 0), with its water, stream and spray. Used twice:

- `ram`: `valve.assembly` is ONE registered part, `waste-valve` (its 14 parts are its meshes; the water and spray are not).
  The plate carries the studs of the waste flange (`hardware: 'ram'`); the section caps belong to `waste-valve`.
- `valve`: the 14 groups are registered one by one, `hardware: 'bench'` (the plate alone), each cap belongs to its part.

Same builders and same `update(state, dt, frozen)`: the tests compare the 14 boxes of both instances to the micron in four
poses. Built in the closed pose; `liftFrames` are lowered by the lift when it opens, the fins turn `spinFrames` (hotspot
anchors never turn), the weight and nuts ride `stackFrames` so that they land on the guide.

**Mechanics** (research §3; lengths are estimates from the fitting names, but the 7 to 8 cm nipple): plate with a Ø 26 bore
and four Ø 9 holes, welded 26×34 socket and bead, reducer 26×34 to 15×21, 15×21 nipple threaded over 7.5 cm with four ports
and its lock nut, female-female sleeve, thermowell guide (Ø 12 × 10 tube, collar whose end face stops the weight), seat milled
at 45 degrees in the foot of the nipple (drawn as its own ring), stainless head, M6 stem, two sleeves 9.9 mm in the 10 mm bore,
fins nut under the head, rebar weight, stroke nut, lock nut. Seat, head and fins sit inside the reducer: seen in section,
X-ray, isolate or exploded.

## The `valve` world

- Plate on two steel parallels on a wooden plinth, a 30 cm steel rule, the paper floor; metres, `minDistance` 4 cm. Hotspots:
  6 main (plate, reducer, nipple, guide, weight, stem) and 8 minor; none has `goto` (`partLinks` links `waste-valve`).
- **Explode** (`explode.ts`, computed from the open-pose extents, 12 mm between parts): weight, nuts, guide, sleeves, sleeve,
  nipple, reducer and socket go up, the stem stays threaded through reducer, nipple and sleeve; seat, head and fins nut leave
  downwards between the parallels (the 18.6 mm head cannot pass the 15 mm bore). The assembly guide plays these offsets for
  the current part, so they are the insertion paths.
- **Section**: plate, socket, reducer, nipple (ports crossed), sleeve, guide, seat and head are cut and capped; stem, sleeves,
  fins, weight and nuts stay whole, as in a drawing. **Flux**: dots from under the plate through the seat out of a port.
  Water, stream, spray and dots are clipped by the section (dots 3 mm behind the plane, so those on the axis stay).

## The `ram` world

Its waste valve is the model above. New: `inlet-flange` (pipe stub, flange with hub, 2 mm gasket, four M10 bolts into the
strainer's flange; still starts at `STUB_X0`), `delivery-union` (three-piece union, compression nut, PE stub; the pipe still
ends at `OUT_X`), `foundation` (60 mm concrete pad under the feet with four M10 studs through the lugs, washers and nuts; its
top is `RAM.floorY`, its underside the floor of the world, `FOUNDATION_BOTTOM`). Explode (`ram/explode.ts`): all but the
foundation are lifted 70 mm off the studs, then go along their axis; the outlet valve and union follow the chamber.

## What `update(state, dt, view)` reads

`wasteValve`, `stroke`, `strokeCount`: stem, fins, weight, nuts (drawn closing 12 ms, opening 30 ms; stem turns 14 degrees
per stroke, at most 160 degrees/s; stroke drawn x4, 30 mm at most). `wasteJetFlow`: stream, spray, Flux speed. The ram also
reads the check valve, chamber, gauge, snifter and flows. `view.cutaway`, `view.schematic`; `slowMo` is not applied again.
While anything is exploded, hidden or wire, the valve is held open and the water hidden.
